Frequently asked questions

Do I need a credit card to sign up?

No. The Free For Life plan needs no payment information. Sign up and start using Stethescope immediately.

Can I upgrade or downgrade anytime?

Yes. Change plans anytime from your dashboard. Pro-rated billing applies on upgrades.

What payment methods are supported?

UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), credit/debit cards, and net banking via Razorpay. GST invoices issued automatically.

Is my patient data secure?

Yes. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Hosted on Indian servers for compliance.
